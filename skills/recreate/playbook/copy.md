## Writing the page
(from Anthropic frontend-design, taste-skill copy self-audit and Vercel content rules)

- Write from the visitor's side. Name things the way the client's customers would, in plain words. Say what the client does and what the visitor gets.
- Active voice. A button says exactly what happens: "Get my quote", "Book a call", not "Submit" or "Learn more" on every card.
- Specific beats clever. Use the client's real services, location, audience and facts from clientFacts. If a sentence could sit on any competitor's page, rewrite it with something only this client can say.
- Each element does one job: the headline promises, the lead explains, the card names one benefit and backs it with one fact or detail.
- Numerals for counts ("3 steps", "24 hours"). Sentence case for headings and buttons unless the competitor's headings are clearly all caps.
- Before returning, re-read every visible string. Rewrite anything grammatically off, vague ("we plan to stay that way"), or that sounds like an AI trying to sound thoughtful.
