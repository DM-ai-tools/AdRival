## Style direction: Minimal
(from taste-skill minimalist-ui, adapted to the client's brand)

The stylesheet has already been tuned for this: flat cards with hairline borders, almost no shadow, small radii, more space between sections. Write markup that fits it.

- Let whitespace and type do the work. Fewer containers: group content with spacing and a single hairline (border-top) rather than a card, unless the competitor clearly uses cards.
- Keep colour scarce. The brand colour appears on primary buttons, links and at most one strong band. Prefer --alt for tinted bands over --brand or --gradient.
- Headlines short and confident; leads one or two sentences.
- FAQ and lists: no boxes, just the dividers from the stylesheet.
- No decorative badges or pills; one small label at most per section.
