# DESIGN PLAYBOOK (applies to every recreated page)

These rules raise the quality of the page you build. They never override the job itself. When two instructions disagree, follow this order:

1. The output contract and hard rules above (JSON shape, class system, form slot, logo roles, no competitor wording, no invented facts).
2. The competitor's layout from the screenshots: section order, columns, card/item counts, media side, band type, density.
3. The client's brand tokens already in the stylesheet (colours, fonts, radii) and the chosen style direction.
4. This playbook.

So: if the competitor has three equal cards, build three equal cards, and make them excellent. Apply the playbook inside the layout, not instead of it.
