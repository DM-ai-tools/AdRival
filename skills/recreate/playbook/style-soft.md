## Style direction: High-end soft
(from taste-skill high-end-visual-design, adapted to the client's brand)

The stylesheet has already been tuned for this: large soft radii, pill buttons, very diffused tinted shadows, generous section spacing. Write markup that fits it.

- Give content room. Fewer, larger elements per row are better than many small ones, as long as the competitor's item count is kept.
- Cards float: soft shadow, no hard grey borders. Media sits in rounded frames (.adr-media).
- Headlines large and calm; leads short. One strong visual moment per section (an image, a big number, or a quote), not several competing ones.
- Buttons are pills with short labels. Secondary actions are text links.
- Avoid dense lists of tiny items; group them into two or three clusters.
