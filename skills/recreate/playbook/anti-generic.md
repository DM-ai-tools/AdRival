## Do not look AI-generated
(from Impeccable's detector catalogue, taste-skill section 9 and Anthropic frontend-design)

These are the tells that make a page read as generated. Avoid them unless the competitor's screenshot clearly shows the same device in the same place.

Visual
- No gradient text on headings or numbers. Text is a solid colour.
- No thick coloured stripe on one side of a card (side-tab border).
- No glowing shadows, neon outlines, or purple-to-cyan washes. Shadows come from the stylesheet only.
- No decorative dots before list items, nav links or badges.
- No fake product UI built from divs (fake dashboards, fake chat windows, fake terminals). Use an image slot or leave the media out.
- No hand-drawn decorative SVG illustrations or redrawn logos.

Labels and chrome
- Eyebrows (small uppercase labels above headings) are rationed: at most one in every three sections, and only where the competitor's section has one. The headline alone is usually enough.
- Numbered markers (01 / 02 / 03, "Step 1") only when the content really is a sequence, and then the step content is the label ("Share your goals", not "Step 1: Share your goals").
- No "→" appended to every button, no middle-dot strings of meta ("Fast · Local · Trusted"), no "WORD — fragment" labels.
- No version or status stamps, scroll cues, section numbers, or photo-credit captions.

Copy
- No em dashes (—) or en dashes (–) in visible text. Use a full stop, a comma, a colon or brackets. Use a plain hyphen for ranges (9-5, $40-80).
- No filler verbs or buzzwords: elevate, seamless, unleash, unlock, empower, revolutionise, next-gen, game-changer, cutting-edge, world-class, delve, supercharge, "in today's fast-paced world".
- No accenting a single word of a headline in another colour, italic or weight as decoration.
- No fake-precise numbers (98.7%, 4.9x, 12,000+ clients) unless they are in clientFacts.
- No generic names (John Doe, Jane Smith, Acme) and no emoji.
- One copy register for the whole page: plain, specific, confident. Cute wordplay that does not quite make sense is worse than a plain sentence.
