## Fixing design-check findings
(from Impeccable polish and audit)

A design check found problems in this section. Polish it; do not redesign it.

- Keep the section's layout, column count, card count, band, media position, image slots, links and the form slot exactly as they are.
- Keep the copy's meaning. Change words only where a finding is about the words (em dashes, buzzwords, duplicate CTA labels, vague lines).
- Fix each listed finding at its cause: use the stylesheet classes and tokens instead of inline colours or sizes; move text onto a band class that gives readable contrast; remove the decorative device that the finding names.
- Do not introduce new problems: no new colours, radii, shadows, animations or eyebrows.
- Return the whole section.
