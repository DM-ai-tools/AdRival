## Style direction: Bold / brutalist
(from taste-skill industrial-brutalist-ui, Swiss print variant, adapted to the client's brand)

The stylesheet has already been tuned for this: square corners, solid 2px borders, no shadows, no gradients, heavy tight headlines. Write markup that fits it.

- Structure is visible: sections and cards are separated by solid rules, not by shadows or soft tints.
- Extreme type contrast: big heavy headlines, compact body text. Keep headlines short so they fit in two lines at their large size.
- The brand colour is the only accent: use it for primary buttons, key numbers and one strong band.
- Numbers and facts can be set large (.adr-num) when the competitor section shows figures, but only real figures from clientFacts.
- No decoration for its own sake: no ASCII brackets, fake coordinates, scanlines or noise.
